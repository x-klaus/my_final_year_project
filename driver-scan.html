How it works

  1. Ask the student to open their ticket on the app.
  2. Read the reference under the QR code (e.g. #ATB-51491).
  3. Enter it above and tap Verify & Board.
  4. The manifest and dashboard stats update automatically.

Boarding verification is closed while the bus is full or the trip has ended.
