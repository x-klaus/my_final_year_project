Key Features

Everything You Need for a Smooth Commute

Six powerful features built specifically for the ATBU community.

📍

Live GPS Bus Tracking

See exactly where every bus is on the Yelwa–Gubi route in real time. Map updates every 5–10 seconds.

💺

Real-Time Seat Availability

Know how many seats are left before you leave your room. Counts update instantly as students book or cancel.

🎫

Online Seat Booking

Reserve your seat in advance from your phone or laptop. Get a booking reference and board without queuing.

💳

Secure Digital Payments

Pay your fare safely using Paystack or Flutterwave — Nigeria's most trusted payment gateways.

🗳️

Collective Bus Request

No buses at your campus? Start a request and let fellow students vote to dispatch an empty bus from the other campus to your location.

Next release
💰

Digital Wallet

Top up your balance once and pay fares in one tap. Refunds and trip credits are applied to your wallet automatically.

Coming Next

No Bus? Request One Together.

If you're at Yelwa campus and there are no buses available, you won't have to wait alone. In the next release, students will start collective bus requests; others at your campus join in until enough passengers are confirmed, then admin dispatches an available bus from the other campus.
